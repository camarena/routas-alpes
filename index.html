Route A: The Final Master Plan

Incorporating local guidance to prioritize iconic landmarks like Mont Blanc, the Lavaux Vineyards, and Yvoire while maintaining strict daily riding limits.

Day-by-Day Overview

  • Day 1: Queige → Val-Cenis (~135 km | 3.5 hrs): Bypasses industrial Modane to stay in Val-Cenis right at the base of the next morning's passes.
  • Day 2: Val-Cenis → Guillestre (~120 km | 3.5 hrs): Ascends the legendary Col du Galibier and the lunar fields of the Col d'Izoard.
  • Day 3: Guillestre → Susa (~95 km | 2.5 hrs): Crosses via Col de l'Échelle into Italy, providing an early afternoon arrival in Susa.
  • Day 4: Susa → Les Houches / Chamonix (~195 km | 3.5 hrs): Uses the Maurienne Valley bypass north to approach the Mont Blanc massif.
  • Day 5: Chamonix → Lavaux Vineyards → Évian (~180 km | 3.5 hrs): Crosses into Switzerland, riding the breathtaking Route de la Corniche balcony road through the UNESCO vineyards before returning to France.
  • Day 6: Évian → Yvoire → Annecy (~110 km | 2 hrs riding): Early morning departure (08:00), stopping at the medieval village of Yvoire for coffee before a direct run to Annecy by 11:30 AM.

Itinéraire A : Le Plan Final

Intégrant les conseils locaux pour privilégier les sites emblématiques comme le Mont-Blanc, les vignobles de Lavaux et Yvoire, tout en respectant des limites de temps de conduite strictes.

  • Jour 5 : Chamonix → Vignobles de Lavaux → Évian (~180 km | 3,5 h) : Traversée en Suisse pour emprunter la Route de la Corniche à travers les vignes en terrasses avant de regagner la France.
